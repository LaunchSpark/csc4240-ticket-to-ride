## Scoring and Rankings

Every bot plays every other bot. For a field of five entrants that produces ten unique pairings, and each pairing runs 100 games so a single unlucky shuffle doesn't decide anything.

A bot's score in one game comes from two places. Finishing a destination route pays out its face value. Holding the longest continuous path at the end of the game adds a fixed bonus on top. Incomplete routes subtract, which is why a bot that claims tickets it can't finish often lands below one that quietly banks a few short connections.

We rank on average score across all games a bot played. When two bots finish within a hair of each other on average, the one who won more of their head-to-head games ranks higher. Ties beyond that are left as ties.

Here's what a finished run looks like for a five-bot field:

| Bot | Avg. Score | Games Won | Win % |
|-----------|-----------:|----------:|------:|
| tunnelrat | 118.4 | 291 / 400 | 72.8 |
| coastliner | 111.9 | 262 / 400 | 65.5 |
| ledger | 104.2 | 205 / 400 | 51.2 |
| shortfuse | 89.7 | 138 / 400 | 34.5 |
| driftwood | 76.5 | 104 / 400 | 26.0 |

`tunnelrat` and `coastliner` sit close on average, but the head-to-head record breaks the order cleanly. `ledger` shows the pattern worth watching for in your own bots: a middling average paired with a near-even win count usually points to a bot that plays it safe, winning small and losing small rather than swinging games.
